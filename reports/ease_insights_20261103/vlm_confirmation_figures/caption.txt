Independent confirmation of the frozen VLM paths on LIBERO. (A) Success proportions for Full, Jac and MSE on four suites, with exact success counts shown out of 50. Every suite-condition group contains 10 tasks and five paired initial states (31–35); no independently sampled case error bars are assumed. (B) Success differences in the two original primary directions and the Goal direction registered prospectively after discovery. Positive values follow the stated registered direction. The displayed 95% percentile task-cluster bootstrap intervals were copied from the completed formal report (10,000 replicates; whole tasks retain their five initial states). All three one-sided exact paired tests have Holm-three adjusted p = 1.00. The original Spatial/Long claim and the supplementary Goal/Long claim were not reproduced; this does not establish path equivalence. No statistics were rerun or directions reselected to make this figure.
